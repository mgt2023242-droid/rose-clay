Our Story

A decade of making Anuradhapura feel a little more beautiful.

Rose & Clay opened in 2015 with two chairs and one promise: treat every guest like the only one on the schedule. A decade on, we're a full studio — hair, skin, bridal, nails and makeup — still run on that same promise.

Our mission is simple: real technique, honest pricing, and a space that feels calm the moment you walk in. Our vision is to be the studio Anuradhapura recommends first, for every occasion from a Tuesday trim to a wedding day.
